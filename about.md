# About SonshipResources

This site showcases the elegant Forty Jekyll theme with a custom **Plum color scheme**.

Our design uses a sophisticated plum-themed palette:

- **Primary Plum**: #8e4585
- **Dark Plum**: #6b3564
- **Lavender**: #d4a5d4
