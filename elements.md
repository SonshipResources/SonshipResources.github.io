<h2 id="content">Jesus Christ Is Lord</h2>
<p>Welcome. The heart of the Christian faith is not a program, but a Person: Jesus Christ, crucified for our sins and raised in power. We are saved by grace through faith in Him, and we are called to walk in love, truth, repentance, and hope as we follow Him day by day.</p>

<div class="row">
	<div class="6u 12u$(small)">
		<h3>What We Hold Fast</h3>
		<ul>
			<li>Jesus is Lord, fully God and fully man.</li>
			<li>Scripture is God-breathed and trustworthy.</li>
			<li>Salvation is by grace through faith, not by works.</li>
			<li>The Holy Spirit renews, convicts, and empowers believers.</li>
			<li>Christ will return, and His kingdom will have no end.</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
	<div class="6u$ 12u$(small)">
		<h3>How We Respond</h3>
		<ul class="alt">
			<li>Repent and believe the gospel.</li>
			<li>Pray faithfully and depend on God.</li>
			<li>Read and obey the Word of God.</li>
			<li>Love one another in humility and truth.</li>
			<li>Share the hope of Christ with others.</li>
		</ul>
	</div>
</div>

		<blockquote>
			“Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved.”
			<br />— Acts 16:31
		</blockquote>
